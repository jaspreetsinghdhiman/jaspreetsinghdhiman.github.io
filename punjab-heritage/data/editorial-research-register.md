# Punjab Heritage — Editorial Research Register

This file is an internal editorial working record. It is not displayed as visitor-facing source credits.

## Research record: Museum of Rural Life of Punjab, Ludhiana
- Status: researched introductory article; individual collection-object claims still require object-level verification.
- Institution: Museum of Rural Life / Museum of Social History and Rural Life of Punjab, Punjab Agricultural University, Ludhiana.
- PAU's published material describes the museum as preserving rural household, agricultural, craft and cultural objects, including gaggar, charsa, chulha, chakki, agricultural implements, bronze utensils, musical instruments, harnesses and decorations.
- PAU material identifies the museum as established/built in 1974.
- Public descriptions identify collection material gathered from places including Rahon, Zira, Sunam, Goindwal and Sultanpur Lodhi. Preserve this as a sourced general statement; do not assign an origin village to a specific object without its catalogue record.
- Phulkari research confirms a broader Punjab geography, multiple forms and motifs, and a history extending into the nineteenth century and earlier. Avoid presenting one origin theory or one motif as settled fact.
- Do not infer exact age, maker, village, caste/community, ritual meaning or material from an image alone.

## Editorial cautions
- Keep Charda Punjab and Lehnda Punjab represented with equal care; distinguish historical Punjab from modern political boundaries.
- Separate established facts, institutional descriptions, oral testimony and hypotheses.
- When regional variants are not documented, state that variation exists and leave specifics unassigned.
- No copied paragraphs. Write original explanations from multiple references and maintain this register.
