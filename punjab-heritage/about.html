WHY WE ARE HERE

Rooted in memory.
Built for tomorrow.

A digital home for the objects, knowledge, craft and voices that shape Punjab's cultural identity.

OUR PURPOSE

Preservation begins
with attention.

Punjab Heritage is envisioned as an accessible, growing digital archive. It brings together traditional objects, their local names, uses, materials, regional variations, photographs and the stories shared by the people who know them.

We believe heritage should be documented with context and respect. A photograph alone is not a complete record: the maker, place, language, purpose and source matter too.

OUR PRINCIPLES

01

Authenticity

Record what is known, identify what is uncertain, and avoid presenting assumptions as facts.

02

Community

Make space for local knowledge, family memories, artisans and village communities.

03

Accessibility

Present information in Punjabi and English, with clear navigation and inclusive design.

04

Continuity

Connect generations by making traditional knowledge easier to discover and share.
