Customs are living practices. Their meanings can differ between families, regions, faith communities and generations, so records should include who shared the account and where it is practised.

01

Lohri — ਲੋਹੜੀ

A winter folk festival observed in many Punjabi communities, with regional and family variations.

HERITAGE RECORD
02

Teej / Teeyan — ਤੀਆਂ

A seasonal celebration associated with the monsoon, women’s gatherings, song and dance in many Punjabi communities.

HERITAGE RECORD
03

Wedding traditions — ਵਿਆਹ ਦੀਆਂ ਰਸਮਾਂ

Ceremonies and customs connected with marriage, which differ by family, region and faith.

04

Harvest gatherings — ਫ਼ਸਲ ਦੀਆਂ ਖੁਸ਼ੀਆਂ

Community practices and celebrations connected with agricultural seasons and harvests.
