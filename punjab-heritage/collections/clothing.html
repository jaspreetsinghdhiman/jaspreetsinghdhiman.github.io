A LIVING TRADITION

Knowledge carried
through generations.

Traditional textiles carry knowledge through pattern, colour, material and technique. This collection will document regional names, makers, occasions and the stories behind each textile.

01

Phulkari — ਫੁਲਕਾਰੀ

An embroidered textile tradition associated with Punjab. Motifs, techniques and meanings vary by region, maker and family.

HERITAGE RECORD
02

Khaddar — ਖੱਦਰ

A hand-spun or hand-woven cloth tradition whose local production and use should be documented with regional context.

HERITAGE RECORD
03

Pagg — ਪੱਗ

A turban and an important element of dress, with styles and ways of tying that vary across communities and occasions.

HERITAGE RECORD
04

Paranda — ਪਰਾਂਦਾ

A decorative hair accessory, often braided or woven, with regional forms and materials.
