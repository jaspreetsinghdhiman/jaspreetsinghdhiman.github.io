THE LAND & ITS PEOPLE

Before the machine,
there was knowledge.

Traditional agriculture depended on close observation of soil, seasons, animals and water. Implements varied across regions and changed over time. This collection will document each tool with its local name, function, material, region and source.

01

Hal — ਹਲ

A traditional plough used to open and turn soil. Document the regional design, materials and animal or human power used.

FIELD IMPLEMENT
02

Panjali — ਪੰਜਾਲੀ

A yoke associated with harnessing draught animals. Local forms and terminology should be verified with regional knowledge holders.

HARNESSING
03

Daati — ਦਾਤੀ

A hand tool used for cutting crops or grass, with shape and name variations across communities.

HARVESTING
04

Suhaga — ਸੁਹਾਗਾ

A traditional field implement drawn across prepared soil to break clods and level the surface.

SOIL PREPARATION
