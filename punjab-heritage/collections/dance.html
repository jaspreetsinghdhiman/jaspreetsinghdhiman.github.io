A LIVING TRADITION

Stories rooted
in place & memory.

Dance and performance are shaped by season, work, celebration, gender, region and community. This archive distinguishes stage adaptations from community practices wherever sources allow.

01

Bhangra — ਭੰਗੜਾ ↗

A vigorous Punjabi dance tradition strongly associated with harvest celebrations and now performed in many community and stage settings.

FULL ARTICLE
02

Giddha — ਗਿੱਧਾ ↗

A women’s folk dance in which boliyan, gestures, rhythm and group participation are central.

FULL ARTICLE
03

Jhumar — ਝੂਮਰ ↗

A graceful dance tradition associated with western Punjab and neighbouring regions, with local styles and variations.

FULL ARTICLE
04

Kikli — ਕਿੱਕਲੀ ↗

A paired folk movement traditionally performed by girls and women, often accompanied by song.

FULL ARTICLE
05

Boliyan — ਬੋਲੀਆਂ ↗

Short folk verses used in dance and social gatherings, carrying humour, observation and local memory.
