COLLECTION 02 · HOME & EVERYDAY LIFE

Ghar & Rasoi.

Objects that shaped the rhythm of the Punjabi home — from preparing food to gathering together.

THE HEART OF THE HOME

Everyday objects.
Generational stories.

Kitchen and household objects reveal how families cooked, stored water, prepared grains and shared meals. Materials and names varied by region, household and period.

01

Chulha — ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ

A traditional cooking hearth, often built from earth or masonry and adapted to local fuel and household needs.

COOKING
02

Madhaani — ਮਧਾਣੀ

A hand-operated churning tool used in preparing butter from curd. Designs and operation differed by locality.

FOOD PREPARATION
03

Gagar — ਗਾਗਰ

A vessel traditionally used for carrying or storing water, made in different materials and forms.

STORAGE
04

Paraat — ਪਰਾਤ

A wide, shallow vessel used for kneading dough and other household food preparation.
