A LIVING TRADITION

Knowledge carried
through generations.

Games are a record of social life: they show how children and adults played, competed and gathered. Rules and names may differ from village to village.

01

Gulli-danda — ਗੁੱਲੀ ਡੰਡਾ

A traditional stick-and-piece game played in open spaces.

HERITAGE RECORD
02

Bante — ਬੰਟੇ

A game played with small glass or clay marbles, with many local rules and names.

HERITAGE RECORD
03

Kabaddi — ਕਬੱਡੀ

A team contact sport with deep roots in Punjabi rural sporting culture.

HERITAGE RECORD
04

Pithu / Satoliya — ਪਿੱਠੂ

A traditional team game involving a stack of flat pieces and a ball; local versions vary.
