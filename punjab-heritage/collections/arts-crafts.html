A LIVING TRADITION

Craft traditions connect material knowledge with patience, place and skilled hands. Records should identify the artisan, community, technique, tools and source.

01

Thathera craft — ਠਠੇਰਾ ਕਲਾ

Traditional metalworking associated with shaping and finishing vessels from metals such as brass and copper. Local practice and artisan attribution are essential.

HERITAGE RECORD
02

Pottery — ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦੇ ਭਾਂਡੇ

Clay vessels and objects shaped for domestic, storage and ritual uses, with forms linked to local clay and firing practices.

HERITAGE RECORD
03

Woodwork — ਲੱਕੜ ਦਾ ਕੰਮ

Carving, joinery and functional wooden objects made by skilled craftspeople.

HERITAGE RECORD
04

Weaving — ਬੁਣਾਈ

Handwoven textiles and utility items made using local fibres, looms and techniques.
