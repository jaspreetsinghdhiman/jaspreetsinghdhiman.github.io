Traditional transport reflects local roads, agricultural work, animal husbandry and changing technology. Forms varied by time and region.

01

Bail-gaddi — ਬੈਲ ਗੱਡੀ

A bullock cart used for carrying people, crops and goods.

02

Tanga — ਟਾਂਗਾ

A horse-drawn carriage used for passenger travel in many towns and rural areas.

Cycle — ਸਾਈਕਲ

The bicycle became an important form of personal transport and everyday mobility.

HERITAGE RECORD

Camel cart — ਊਠ ਗੱਡੀ

A cart drawn by a camel in areas where this animal was part of local transport and trade.
