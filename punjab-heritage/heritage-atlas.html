THE FIVE HISTORIC RIVERS

A land shaped
by five waters.

The name Punjab is commonly explained through the Persian words for “five” and “water”. The historic river system links places now divided by an international border; present-day river flows and water arrangements must be described separately from the older cultural geography.

River and treaty context: Government of Punjab and Bhakra Beas Management Board.
01 / LANDSCAPE

Water is history, livelihood and ecology.

Rivers, canals, wetlands and seasonal streams have shaped settlement, agriculture, trade and everyday life. The archive will record both heritage and contemporary environmental change without treating the river system as a static map.
