## Subject-matching notes

- Use the 1899 and 1908–1927 materials only as historical archival imagery, not as photographs of present-day Punjab.
- A bullock-cart image must not be reused for a tractor, ploughing technique, or a named village.
- A Phulkari textile image belongs in clothing / textile / craft contexts, not games.
- A chullah photograph belongs in traditional cooking / household contexts.
- A village-life photograph must not be captioned as a specific village unless its source confirms that location.
- Confirm the depicted side of Punjab (today's Indian Punjab or Pakistani Punjab) before assigning it to Charda or Lehnda Punjab pages.
