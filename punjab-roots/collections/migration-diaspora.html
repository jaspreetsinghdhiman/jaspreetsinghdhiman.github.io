Migration & Punjabi Diaspora

Trace the many journeys that have shaped Punjabi families, communities and cultural life across the world.

A RESEARCH-LED COLLECTION

Understanding the living heritage.

Migration has shaped Punjab across generations. People have moved for work, education, trade, military service, family, safety and new opportunities. These journeys include internal movement, colonial-era migration, Partition displacement and contemporary global diasporas. Each story deserves its own time, place and voice.

How to read this collection

These are editorial introductions, not claims that every practice is identical across Punjab. Regional names, dates and customs vary. Individual records should be supported by reliable references, local knowledge and community review.

Punjabi terms are shown in Gurmukhi where appropriate. Shahmukhi and regional vocabulary can be added through reviewed contributions.

01

Movement before and during Partition ↗

Before 1947, people moved across towns, villages and regions for commerce, study, service and family life. Partition brought mass displacement, violence, loss and new beginnings. Personal testimonies must be handled with consent, careful chronology and sensitivity to different experiences.

02

Work, education and global networks ↗

Punjabi migration to East Africa, the United Kingdom, Canada, the United States and other destinations developed through different historical routes. Work, education, family networks and changing immigration systems shaped these movements; avoid treating the diaspora as one uniform community.

FULL ARTICLE
03

Language, food, music and identity abroad ↗

Diaspora communities sustain and transform Punjabi through gurdwaras, cultural associations, schools, media, music, food businesses, festivals and family life. New generations may use Punjabi alongside other languages and create new cultural forms.

FULL ARTICLE
04

Ethical oral-history practice ↗

Ask permission before recording or publishing. Let narrators review names, dates and sensitive passages. Explain where recordings will appear, allow withdrawal where feasible, avoid publishing private addresses or identifying details, and distinguish remembered experience from independently verified fact.
