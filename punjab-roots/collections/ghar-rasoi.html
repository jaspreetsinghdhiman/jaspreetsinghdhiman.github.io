THE HEART OF THE HOME

Everyday objects.
Generational stories.

Kitchen and household objects reveal how families cooked, stored water, prepared grains and shared meals. Materials and names varied by region, household and period.

01

Chulha — ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ↗
