THE BIGGER PICTURE

Understanding the context.

Punjab’s past has been shaped by people working in many fields: political movements, education, literature, agriculture, social reform, spiritual life, art and public service.

What this theme includes

A responsible profile looks beyond a famous name. It places a person in their time, explains the movement or institution they were part of, identifies the sources behind key claims and recognises the people and communities who worked alongside them.

Look closer

Punjab’s history crosses present-day borders and includes different languages, religions, political positions and experiences. Profiles should reflect that breadth without flattening disagreement or turning complex lives into slogans.

How we document it

Dates, affiliations, quotations and attributions are checked against reliable records wherever possible. Uncertain details are labelled rather than repeated as established fact.

Punjab’s freedom struggle was not one story

People from the wider historic Punjab took part in different movements and forms of public action, including anti-colonial organising, the Ghadar movement, the Akali reform movements, peasant and workers’ organising, education, journalism and constitutional politics. Their aims, methods and political beliefs were not identical. A responsible archive places each person in the specific movement, place and period supported by the evidence.

Names need records, not just remembrance

Government publications such as the Ministry of Culture’s Dictionary of Martyrs and historical works produced by Punjabi University provide starting points for checking names, dates and documented activities. District histories can add local context. These sources have their own scope and limitations; an entry in one catalogue should not be treated as a complete account of a person’s life or as proof that every person shared the same political outlook.

Editorial promise

Punjab Roots labels oral memories, historical records and interpretation clearly. If a detail is uncertain or contested, that uncertainty stays visible.
