about me

The developer behind the code

statusopen to work

Hi, I'm Jaspreet — a frontend developer based in Punjab, India, with a habit of caring too much about the small details: the spacing between two buttons, the timing of a hover animation, whether a form actually feels good to fill out.

I started out customizing WordPress themes for local businesses, which taught me how real clients think about their websites — not in terms of frameworks, but in terms of trust, speed, and whether it looks "professional." That's still the lens I build through today, even as my toolkit has grown to include React, custom design systems, and full Figma-to-code workflows.

These days, most of my work sits at the intersection of design and development: I take a Figma file (sometimes my own, sometimes a designer's), and turn it into a website that's fast, responsive, accessible, and genuinely pleasant to use — not just a pixel-perfect copy, but something that feels alive.

When I'm not building interfaces, I'm usually breaking my own side-projects on purpose, just to learn how to fix them properly.
